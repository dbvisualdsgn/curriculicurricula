# Changelog

## Unreleased

### A more compact CV
- The summary sits right under the name and contacts, without its own rule and title.
- Skills are in two columns: Core skills and Stack & tools, each with its own rule and title.
- The PDF follows the same layout and shows the contact icons.
- One PDF download, as on the page: the "ATS plain" version is gone.
- Work experience is the same size as education and certifications: smaller titles, logos and spacing.

### Appearance
- Each CV keeps its look: the appearance and the typefaces it was edited in come back when you open it, from My CVs or from a link, and travel in the PDF.
- The Appearance panel is simpler: no descriptions, no reset.
- The CV is always a little lighter than the page around it, in the same tone. The PDF uses that lighter colour too.
- The Background button is now Appearance, in the CV menu and the site menu: Classic, Paper or Color.
- Classic is white paper, black text and photos in colour. Paper is the e-reader look, as before.
- Color lets you pick any tone for the background and the text, or start from six presets. The background stays light and the text stays dark, so it always reads well. Photos and highlights take the colours too.
- New photos are kept in colour; the page shows them grey, in colour or in two colours. Photos added before stay grey.
- The "As on the page" PDF follows the appearance.

### A clearer CV menu
- Every tool for your CV sits in one block: Edit, Recruiter check, Fonts, Background and Delete, each with a name.
- Delete asks before it removes anything. Share and Download stay on the right.
- The language moved up, next to Example CVs. The About page is gone: the footer says who made the site.

### Share with one link
- Public and Private are gone, and so is the Public CVs page. Your CV stays in your browser.
- Share makes one link that carries your CV inside it. Nothing is stored online.
- The link shows your CV as it is now. Changed it? Share a new link.
- The examples page is now called Example CVs.

### Humbler, and to the point
- The homepage says what the tool is: a clear CV in one standard format, from a PDF you have or a blank page.
- How it works comes first, then private or shared, then the examples. The recruiter check and job-ad tips are extras.
- The Why page tells one idea: a CV should be a standard format, not a design project. No moving words, no counters.
- Who made it is now in the footer, as a short CV with email and LinkedIn.

### More examples, and an About page
- 34 example CVs, from first jobs to senior roles: waiter, delivery rider, student, care assistant, bus driver, head chef, CTO and more.
- Filter the examples by level: first job, experienced, or senior and lead.
- A new About page says who made the site. The Why page keeps to the idea.

### Clearer, and honest when something goes wrong
- Error messages are easier to read: dark text with a small red dot.

### The CV menu
- Three groups: write and check, how the page reads, who gets it. Download is the main button.
- Delete is in My CVs now, away from Download.
- The recruiter check score stays readable while the check is open.

### Starting a CV
- Your first visit opens the editor, with your name first.
- The editor looks like the page: the same labels, rules and quiet fields.

### Import, check and tailor
- Importing a PDF now fills every section: profile, skills, experience, education and languages. It reads more
  heading styles, LinkedIn’s PDF, and CVs that put the dates above each job.
- The recruiter check adds up: an empty CV scores 0, and each tip shows the points it adds.
- Pasting a job ad has its own frame and button, under the check.
- The editor opens from the left. Import and export as JSON, copy as text and Skim view are gone.

### Phones and keyboards
- A Menu on phones, so your CV and the other pages are always one tap away.
- Bigger buttons, and nothing runs off the screen on small phones.
- The example CVs on the homepage scroll into view when you reach them with the keyboard.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-19

The first release of curriculi *curricula*: a free CV tool, with no account, in 7 languages.

A passport is designed once, with care, the same for everyone. A CV usually isn’t. This release makes the CV a
document too: one template, designed once and well, free for everyone.

### A CV, like a document
- One clear CV sheet. You write; the design is already done.
- Next to your name: nothing, your initials in a black square, or a photo. The PDF follows the same choice.
- Choose the typefaces, the language and the light of the paper. Text keeps strong contrast at every setting.
- The whole site and the CV headings are in English, Italian, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese and Dutch.

### Write and tailor
- A simple editor in 4 tabs. Your CV saves as you type.
- Keep several CVs in the same browser, and save named versions for different jobs.
- Import a PDF you already have. Two-column layouts and spaced-out headings are read section by section.
- Import and export JSON, or copy your CV as plain text for application forms.

### Check
- The recruiter check scores your CV and lists fixes in plain words, each with the points it costs.
- It opens only when you ask, from the CV menu.
- Paste a job ad to see its job title, and the keywords your CV has or is missing.
- Skim view shows what a recruiter takes in on a first pass.

### Share, or keep it private
- Every CV starts private: it stays in this browser.
- Make it public to get a link and an embed code. A copy is saved online while it’s public.
- Make it private again and that copy is deleted.
- Showing it on the Public CVs page is a separate choice.

### Download
- “As on the page”: the same layout, fonts, logos and photo.
- “ATS plain”: one column of plain text, for job portals.

### The site
- A new homepage with live examples of the recruiter check and of private and public.
- A new page, “Why”: one person’s passport, driving licence and CV, and where the project came from.
- 17 example CVs from many fields, in the current layout.

### Accessible
- Tested against WCAG 2.2 AA, with AAA contrast for text on the paper.
- Everything that moves can be paused, and nothing moves with “reduce motion” on.

### Worth knowing
- The code moved to github.com/dbvisualdsgn/curriculicurricula. The site address follows the repository name,
  so links shared with the old `/Careerpath/` address may stop working.
- The recruiter check helps your CV read well. It doesn’t promise a job: applying early and being introduced by
  someone count too.
